# Sixers Seats: decisions from the Oct 20 grilling

## Selling plan
- **Goal:** make back $8,000, the price of both seats for the season. Count only money we actually receive:
  - friend sales at the price paid
  - Ticketmaster payouts after TM's 10% cut
- **Ticketmaster:** we decide game by game which games go to Ticketmaster. The app assumes a cap of **10** resales until the real number is confirmed. The rest are sold person to person.
- **Tranches:** three, each with a decision deadline.

| Tranche | Dates | Decide by |
|---|---|---|
| T1 | Oct 22 – Dec 23 | **Sun Nov 1** |
| T2 | Jan 4 – Feb 11 | Mon Dec 7 |
| T3 | Feb 17 – Apr 11 | Wed Jan 20 |

## Pricing
- **Price steps:** Stretch (until 14 days out) → **14 days** → **5 days** → **2 days**. This is the current ladder, renamed.
- **Friend price:** based on the comp, i.e. what a similar pair costs on Vivid with fees included. The Ticketmaster list price is the comp ÷ 1.2.
- **Friends & family:** 20% off the friend price by default, adjustable, **rounded down to $5**.
- **How we quote it:** "2 tickets, $X each", always sold as a pair.
- **"Come with me" offer:** a message variant that says "I'll come with you, you just cover your seat." It's priced as one ticket at the F&F rate.

## Contacts
- **Lists:** separate lists for Phil and Andrew. Each is its own tab in the shared sheet, and the app shows you only your own. Either of you can still open the other's tab directly in Google Sheets.
- **Fields:** name, phone, email, group. No outreach status.
- **Ways to add people:** paste a list, add one at a time, or upload a vCard/CSV export from your phone or iCloud.

## Messages
- **Writing:** Claude writes everything up front. That's 40 researched hooks plus templates for each message type and variant. The app fills in the game, date, price and contact name.
- **Live "Remix with Claude":** later. It needs a small server holding an API key.
- **Hooks:** real storylines, researched. No invented roster facts.
- **Message types:** Text, Email, and Community board post (neighborhood, alumni, Reddit, Slack/Discord).
- **Buttons:**
  - Copy
  - Open in Messages, prefilled with the contact's number and the text
  - Open email, prefilled with subject and body
- **Logging a sale:** the buyer is picked from contacts or typed in.

## Tracking
- **Thermometers:** revenue toward $8,000, and games sold out of all 40.
- **When a sale counts:** only once the money is in **and** the tickets are transferred. Nothing pending is shown.
- **Row colors:** each game row is colored by days to the game (more than 14 / 14–5 / 5–2 / under 2). Tranches show only as the labeled bands.

## Navigation
- **Side rail:** Sell, Strategy, Contacts, Sales, Settings.
- **Older screens:** Home, Games, Market and Split come out of the rail and are reached from Settings.
