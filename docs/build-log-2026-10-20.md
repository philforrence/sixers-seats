Calls I made without asking. Each one is easy to change.

## App
- **Side rail:** Sell, Strategy, Contacts, Sales, Settings. The app opens on **Sell**. Home, Games, Market and Split are under Settings → More views.
- **Price steps are renamed** Stretch / 14 days / 5 days / 2 days. Saved overrides carry over (same columns).
- **Friend price** = the Ticketmaster list price × 1.2, rounded to $5. That's about what similar seats cost online with fees included. If you override a step price, the friend price follows it.
- **F&F** = friend price minus 20%, rounded down to $5. The % is editable per pitch; the default lives in Settings.
- **"Come with me"** is priced at one seat at the F&F rate.
- **Board posts** always use the regular price, since they're public. The dialog says so.
- **Logging a "come with me" sale** (1 ticket) keeps the game as "going", with you and the buyer in the seats. It adds to revenue, but the "games sold" meter only counts a game when both seats are sold.
- **Ticketmaster cap is now 10.** A one-time update changes your saved setting from 7 to 10. Tranche 1 is locked by Sun Nov 1 (Settings → Fixed decide-by dates).
- **Days-out colors:** a stripe plus a light tint per row. Yellow = 14–5 days, orange = 5–2, red = last 2 days. Tranches are now only the labeled bands.
- **Contacts:**
  - Each person's list is its own tab in the sheet ("Contacts - Phil"). The tab is created the first time you open Contacts.
  - Adding the same name + phone twice is skipped.
  - The CSV import understands Google Contacts and iCloud column names.
  - Demo mode has 16 made-up people (555 numbers, example.com emails) for screenshots.
- **Messages:**
  - Each type and offer has 2–3 phrasings; **Next wording** cycles through them.
  - **Picking a contact** swaps in their first name. Names like "Aunt Bev" or "Luis & Ana" are kept whole.
  - **Hand edits are kept** when you switch contacts; only the greeting name changes.
  - **Remix with Claude** shows a "coming later" note.
- **Sales:** a sale counts once **both** "money received" and "tickets transferred" are checked. Unfinished sales show in the log but not in the meters.
- **Hooks** (data/hooks.json) were researched by a Claude subagent: 30 high confidence, 10 medium.
  - The medium ones depend on a star actually playing. Each game has an alternate hook that doesn't, and you can edit any hook in the pitch dialog (it saves per game).
  - Verified headline facts: LeBron signed (2 years), Jaylen Brown came in by trade, and Embiid, Maxey and Edgecombe are all back.
  - Ben Simmons (Kings) tore his Achilles on Oct 18, so his hook was avoided.
